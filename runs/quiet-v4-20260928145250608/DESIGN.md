# Folio — design record

## Before implementation

The product is a place to work through a question, with documents as context. All conversation, draft, attachment, and response state remains in the supplied `useChat()` hook. This record describes design judgment, not measured causal evidence.

Three approaches considered:

1. **A casework cabinet:** persistent history rail, document shelf, central conversation. It favors frequent switching, but the supplied model has no independent document library and the rail would permanently compete with reading.
2. **A research notebook:** a wide editorial page with numbered questions and a separate margin for activity. It gives answers a strong hierarchy, but margin notes become awkward on phones and numbering adds meaning the model does not have.
3. **A quiet research desk — selected:** one white work surface, a compact current-conversation disclosure, open prose, and one soft writing surface. Actual recent conversations help resume work on the empty view. Document metadata stays with the question it informed. This serves both starting and reading without inventing projects or extra capabilities.

Composition commitment: the input, questions, answers, and recent-work rows share a left edge in a 760px reading column. A small typographic Folio wordmark sits at the outer edge. The primary shape is a moderately rounded rectangle. History originates at the current-conversation control. The important object transition is a real attachment leaving the draft and appearing with its submitted question, retaining its ID, filename, and size.

## Scope and conflicts

All five selected entrypoints were read. Modern Interface Craft sets composition; Apple Design informs interaction instead of importing another visual shell. Its initial-response-only instruction does not apply to this specific build request. Accessibility and the behavioral contract override any aesthetic hiding of currently required controls. Motion Storytelling changes the choice to preserve document identity and local cause/effect; its film/storyboard/narration machinery does not apply. Gesture momentum, rubber-banding, sound, and haptics did not change a decision because the app has no corresponding interaction. Nested Geometry does not dictate the palette or page layout. No additional skills, assets, research, or dependencies are used.

## Objective repair — focused skip-link overlap

The supplied validation report shows four passing interaction tests and a mobile timeout: after Tab focuses “Skip to workspace,” its fixed overlay intercepts clicks on “Open conversations.” This is interaction evidence, not a new visual refinement session.

Decision before repair: keep the native skip link as the first keyboard target, move it inside the already positioned header, and reveal it immediately below that header using its actual height. The alternative considered was disabling pointer events on the link; that would prevent clicking a visible link, so it was rejected. Accessibility “Skip links (2.4.1),” “Focus visible (2.4.7),” and `references/A11Y-PATTERNS.md#skip-link` support retaining a visible, operable native link. The reference's top-edge placement needs adaptation to avoid the demonstrated overlap. Implementation: `src/App.tsx` and `.skip-link` in `src/styles.css`. Header geometry, normal resting views, color tokens, and hook behavior remain unchanged. All five selected entrypoints were read for this repair; Modern Interface Craft, Apple Design, Nested Geometry, and Motion Storytelling did not independently change this targeted decision.

Repair verification: `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm build --configLoader runner` passed (the build retains its chunk-size advisory). `pnpm test:e2e` was attempted but could not start its preview server: the sandbox rejected Vite's temporary config write under `node_modules/.vite-temp` with EPERM. No browser tests executed in this repair session, and no sandbox workaround was attempted. External runner verification of the repaired Tab → Open conversations → Document review sequence remains pending. Protected source, fixtures, tests, package files, and configuration were not edited.

## Browser-informed refinement 1 — critique before changes

All five selected skill entrypoints were reread, along with the light-workspace and interaction-recipes references and the relevant accessibility patterns. The supplied ten screenshots/contact sheets are the rendered evidence; the two named videos were not available inside this workspace and were not viewed. Temporal samples show states, not smoothness or velocity.

The three most consequential weaknesses, identified before editing:

1. **Mobile reading loses useful information.** `mobile-research.png` clips the Owner column, and the mobile contact sheet repeats that cut during streaming. This is local table overflow, even though the report correctly finds no page overflow. The source forces at least 384px of table columns into a roughly 320px reading space. Excess reserved scrollbar gutters reduce available space. The prose, table, and input also use inconsistent content edges. Preserve open prose, but give tables the full work-surface width and let cell text wrap.
2. **Returning to work is less clear than starting it.** `desktop-welcome.png` and `desktop-research.png` truncate short current-thread titles despite ample room; the intermediate header is disconnected from the work column. Both history selection and recent-work selection currently focus the textarea, a source-confirmed risk of opening the mobile keyboard when the person wants to read. This cannot be established from the settled screenshots alone. Give the title real available width and distinguish reading an existing thread from starting a new one.
3. **Recovery and completion need steadier feedback.** The contact sheets show useful stop/retry states, but source inspection finds that Stop and Retry can remove the focused button without an intentional destination. Busy is also the only reason to keep an unfocused composer toolbar visible, so completion can change dock height. In `mobile-activity.png`, the activity count is detached from its label; the expanded source continues to paint a running dot after stopping. Make the status relationship clearer, preserve the engaged surface through completion, and give explicit recovery actions a stable focus destination.

The white canvas, modest wordmark, quiet initial composer, real recent items, readable response hierarchy, attachment metadata, and nonmodal history earn their place. No sidebar, greeting, extra explanation panel, or decorative motion is warranted. Supplied observations report no runtime errors, no page overflow, no automated accessibility findings, visible retry after cancellation, and a visible reduced-motion composer. Those findings confirm useful existing choices; they do not establish full keyboard, screen-reader, copy, or motion correctness.

### Interpretation and verification limits

Modern Interface Craft still owns the light art direction. Its quiet resting input is reconciled with explicit, stable controls during an active writing/response session; that is engagement state, not a permanent empty toolbar. Apple Design's glass surfaces, gesture physics, and platform font preference add no new decision in this pass. Motion Storytelling informs recovery and state continuity; no film, timed narration, or new animation is added. Nested Geometry confirms the existing shape family rather than causing a cosmetic change. The accessibility report does not contain a failing node to repair; the keyboard refinements are based on identifiable source paths, not a claimed new audit result.
